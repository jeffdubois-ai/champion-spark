The situation in one paragraph
LATAM is one of Amplitude's fastest-growing regions — $11.5M in active pipeline, 121 opportunities, 4 AEs — and it is covered by a single Solutions Engineer. Evandro Pacolla is supporting active POCs across Brazil, Mexico, Argentina, Colombia, Peru, Uruguay, Chile, Ecuador, El Salvador, and the Dominican Republic — in two languages — with ~72 minutes of available SE time per active deal per month at a standard 40-hour week, against a benchmark of 8–12 hours. That is not a prioritization problem — it is a math problem. Comparable well-staffed Amplitude teams cover every active deal with dedicated SE attention. The difference is not product, market, or talent — it is SE coverage. One additional hire corrects the ratio, protects a key person operating at unsustainable load, and positions Amplitude to capture the $1.55M in mega-deals (Itaú, Bradesco, BCP Peru) advancing to S4 in Q3/Q4 with no capacity to receive them.
The ask: +1 SE in LATAM, H2 2026.
